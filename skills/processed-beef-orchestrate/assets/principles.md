# Principles

Template for `docs/principles.md`. Principles are user-owned. Agents may propose
changes, the Orchestrator discusses them with the user, and only the user
approves. No work may contradict an active principle.

- Correctness, quality, simplicity, readability, and maintainability over
  speed.
- Follow upstream best practices and project conventions; prefer existing
  platform capabilities over bespoke code.
- No hacks, shortcuts, speculative abstractions, or unrelated refactors.
- Verify claims with current evidence; never infer success from intent.
- Spend effort in proportion to risk; choose the smallest correct solution.
